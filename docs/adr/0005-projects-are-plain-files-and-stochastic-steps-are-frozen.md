# Projects are plain files; stochastic steps are frozen as artifacts and recorded in a Receipt

State lives in readable files, with no database: `projects/<slug>/` holds the idea, `story.json` and `preset.json`, and each Job under `jobs/<id>/` holds a `manifest.json` Receipt; shared `engine/`, `styles/`, `presets/` and `assets/` sit at the root, and the audio cache is keyed by hash of text plus Voice. The LLM and TTS steps are not deterministic, so their outputs (the Story, the narration audio) are saved and become inputs; re-running a Job replays the frozen artifacts instead of regenerating them. The Receipt records content hashes of the Story, merged config, renderer snapshot, fonts, assets and audio, plus tool builds (Chrome, ffmpeg), engine and Style pack versions, the Voice engine, model and seed, and the Claude account the Job ran under. Reproducibility is guaranteed only where the environment and artifacts match the Receipt; frame hashes verify a re-render but are not a condition of it. Because a Preset is reusable, the same setup can produce many other videos from different Ideas.
