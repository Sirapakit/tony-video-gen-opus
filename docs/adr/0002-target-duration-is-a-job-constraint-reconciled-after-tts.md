# Target duration is a Job constraint, reconciled against real narration audio

Actual duration is only known after the narration audio exists, so Target duration is not derived from the Story alone. The Story generator budgets per Scene from the Target duration; real audio is then generated and the total is checked against the Target with a tolerance. If it is out of tolerance, first adjust Voice speed within a natural-sounding range, then send only the offending Scenes back to the LLM to shorten or extend, and show every adjustment at the review step.

Observation, not a measurement of the `speed` control (one unseeded run of 10 Thai lines with OmniVoice; details on branch `prototype/lemo-headless-and-tts-bench`, `prototype/RESULT.md`): per-line duration ratios vs speed 1.0 ranged 0.92-1.24 at speed 0.9 (ideal 1.11) and 0.81-1.17 at 1.1 (ideal 0.91). Each ratio compares two independently sampled generations, so speed and sampling noise are confounded and no variance can be attributed to `speed` itself. The rule stands either way: a duration adjustment is re-synthesised and re-measured, never predicted. Tolerance is a product policy set independently of any of these numbers.
