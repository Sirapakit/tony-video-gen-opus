# Target duration is a Job constraint, reconciled against real narration audio

Actual duration is only known after the narration audio exists, so Target duration is not derived from the Story alone. The Story generator budgets per Scene from the Target duration; real audio is then generated and the total is checked against the Target with a tolerance. If it is out of tolerance, first adjust Voice speed within a natural-sounding range, then send only the offending Scenes back to the LLM to shorten or extend, and show every adjustment at the review step.

Evidence so far (one local prototype run, OmniVoice on Apple Silicon, 3 Thai lines, 4 seeds): with a fixed seed, `speed` 0.9 and 1.1 changed a line's duration by a median 1.107x and 0.916x (ideal 1.111x and 0.909x; ranges 0.989-1.143 and 0.874-0.954), and the same seed reproduced identical audio. So Voice speed is a usable first lever when the seed is fixed and recorded. Two consequences: the characters-per-second rate used for the Story budget belongs to a saved Voice (a cloned reference voice) and not to a model, because the same model gave rates from about 7.6 to 10.3 characters per second across two runs with different reference voices; and any duration adjustment is still re-synthesised and re-measured, never predicted. Tolerance is a product policy set independently of any of these numbers.
